📖 Chemistry Reviewer

Concentration
Molarity: M = moles / liters of solution
Molality: m = moles / kg solvent
% mass = mass solute / mass solution × 100
% volume = volume solute / volume solution × 100
ppm = mass solute / mass solution × 1,000,000

Chemical Kinetics
Rate = change in concentration / time
Rate law: Rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ

Acids and Bases
pH = −log[H⁺]
pOH = −log[OH⁻]
pH + pOH = 14 at 25°C

Chemical Equilibrium
Kc = products / reactants, each raised to its coefficient
Kp = Kc(RT)ⁿ
Q is calculated like Kc but uses current concentrations.
